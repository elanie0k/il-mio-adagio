The Red Book — Private Edition

Chapter I
The Beginning

There was no prophecy, no council of kings, and no messenger from the stars. There was only a musician, a curious profile, and a stranger whose taste in music was suspiciously perfect.

A chance encounter became a message. A message became a conversation. And before either of them could quite name what was happening, a road had begun.

Perhaps the strangest journeys begin exactly like that. Quietly. Almost by accident.

Chapter II
The Fellowship

Four months since the first page. Three months since the story took its more serious name.

There were strange alphabets. A Queen from Zarveth. A vampire with a castle. And one unfortunate phone that somehow found itself inside a bush.

Fortunately, Dracula Burak was brave enough to cross the fence and retrieve it.

Every great tale needs at least one ridiculous chapter. I am glad ours has one.

Chapter III
The Things I See in You

There are things about you that I admire more than I know how to explain.

Your mercy. Your understanding. Your empathy. The way you can look at someone and try to understand them before judging them.

I love the way your mind works too. You do not simply want to know that something works. You want to know why. You want to understand it, take it apart, solve it, find the answer.

And then there is the quieter part of you. The part that does not belong to everyone. The part that opens up around the people you trust.

I feel lucky that I get to know that person.

Chapter IV
Tales From Distant Lands

There are 839 kilometres between us.

Sometimes that number feels enormous. Sometimes it feels like nothing.

But I have learned something: distance can measure space. It cannot measure love. It cannot measure understanding. And it certainly cannot measure how close one soul can feel to another.

So yes, there are roads between us. But there is also a road that keeps leading me back to you.

Chapter V
The Road Goes Ever On

There is a future I keep imagining.

A home of our own. Decorated by both of us. Cats running through the rooms. Music coming from somewhere in the house. Maybe our own band. Maybe terrible songs. Maybe arguments about which song should be played first.

I want to travel the world with you. I want to see places neither of us has seen. I want to experience things for the first time and immediately look at you because somehow that makes the experience better.

I want mornings beside you. Nights beside you. Ordinary days beside you.

Not all of it has happened yet.

That is the best part.

What I see when I look at you

The Man
Behind the Name

Not a character sheet. Not a list of facts. Just the things I hope you never forget about yourself.

There are many things I could say about you.

I could talk about the things you love, the worlds you disappear into, the music you listen to, the things you create, or all the little details that make you who you are.

But you already know those things.

What you may not always see is the person I see when I look at you.

The Way You Care

One of the things I love most about you is your mercy.

You have a way of understanding people that I deeply admire.

You notice things. You think before you judge. You care about how someone feels.

And somehow, even though you can be quiet around the world, there is this completely different version of you that appears when you are with someone you love.

That version of you is one of my favourite things in the world.

The Way Your Mind Works

I love the way your mind works.

You do not simply want to know that something works.

You want to know why.

You want to understand things. Take them apart. Solve them. Find what is happening underneath the surface.

And I think that is beautiful.

Because your curiosity is not simply something you do. It is part of who you are.

The Strange Familiarity of You

There is something about us that I still find difficult to explain.

It is not simply that we like the same things.

It is not simply that we understand the same jokes, listen to similar music, or get excited about the same strange little things.

It is the feeling that, somehow, our souls speak the same language.

Sometimes I look at you and think:

How can another person feel this familiar?

“Ben insanların arasında yabancıydım, sende kendimi buldum, bu yüzden seni seçtim.”

I don't think I will ever forget you saying that.

Because I think, in my own way, I found something in you too.

A place where I do not have to explain every part of myself.

A person I can laugh with, be strange with, be completely honest with, and simply exist beside.

If I had to describe you in one sentence

You are someone I did not simply fall in love with — you are someone who feels like a part of me that I somehow found in another person.

And yes...

you are also a vampire with a castle.

I suppose every fellowship needs one.

The final chapter — for now

Musica sei, Adagio.

If you ever wonder what you mean to me, I hope you remember this.

I am proud of you.

Not only when you succeed. Not only when you accomplish something. Not only when everything is going right.

I am proud of the person you are, and I will be behind you through every road you choose to take.

You will never be completely alone in this life.

Not while I am here.

There may be 839 kilometres between us now, but there is no distance inside the place you have in my heart.

And one day, these kilometres will be nothing more than a number we remember.

One day I will not have to miss you from far away.

I will simply be there.

I will be there for the ordinary mornings and the ridiculous nights.

For the difficult days. For the celebrations. For the moments when everything goes wrong. For the moments when everything goes right.

For all the versions of life that we have not lived yet.

I want to travel the world with you.

My soul embraced yours.

I love you because you are you.

And I do not want some perfect version of you.

I want you.

And whatever happens on the roads ahead, I want you to know one thing:

I will keep choosing you.

Not because the road is always easy.

But because you are worth the journey.

The road goes ever on.
